## START HERE

*What MY/CO/L is, and your first five minutes with it.*

### WHAT THIS IS

MY/CO/L is the farm terminal for Talamanca Station. It keeps the farm records in one place: the crops, the cultures, the stock, the sales, the power, the room climate and the cash.

It looks like an old green-screen computer on purpose. Everything on it works by tapping or clicking, by keyboard, or by typing short words at the > prompt at the bottom of the screen.

### YOUR FIRST FIVE MINUTES

1. Tap or press any key on the dark start screen. The boot text runs, then the MAIN MENU appears. Any key or tap skips the boot text (unless TUNE > BOOT is set to ALWAYS FULL).
2. On the MAIN MENU, tap a deck to open it. A deck is one part of the farm, like GROW DECK or TRADE DOCK. Its page shows the figures that matter and its stations, one job each.
3. You can also type a deck number or name at the > prompt and press Enter. Try 2 for GROW DECK, or 2.3 to go straight to LOG HARVEST.
4. Look at the top left of any screen for the BACK button. It returns you to the screen you came from, with everything as you left it.
5. Tap ? TIPS in the grey line under the title bar. Now tap a button: instead of pressing it, you get a note saying what it does. Tap ? TIPS again to leave. While tip mode is on, buttons do not press, so nothing saves.
6. Open this guide from MAINFRAME (station 8.1) or type GUIDE. On any screen, GUIDE opens the page for that screen. The DECKS AND STATIONS page explains the whole map.

### WHERE YOUR RECORDS LIVE

Today the app runs in LOCAL MODE. Your records are saved inside this browser on this device. Nothing is sent anywhere. A phone and a laptop each have their own copy until a database is linked later.

Because of that, make a backup often. See the DATA VAULT page. A backup is one file you can keep on a USB stick or send to yourself.

The app works with no internet and no signal. When there is no network the status line says NET DOWN // CACHED, and you can keep working.

### OWNER AND STAFF

The OWNER sees everything. A STAFF login logs the work (harvests, lots, power cuts, readings) and never sees money. Staff do not see pages in this guide for screens they cannot open.

## DECKS AND STATIONS

*How the app is laid out: 8 decks, each with its stations.*

### THE MAP

The app is split into 8 decks, like the decks of a station. Each deck is one part of running the farm. Open a deck and you see its overview: the figures that matter, a few charts, and its stations.

A station is one job, like LOG HARVEST or MONEY OWED. It opens the screen for that job. Every station has a code: 5.6 is deck 5, station 6.

### THE 8 DECKS

- **1 BRIDGE**: Today's work and the whole farm at a glance. Also ASK MY/CO/L and SEARCH.
- **2 GROW DECK**: Block lots from the steam cook to the harvest: the lot board, new lots, harvests, cooks, the run planner and yields.
- **3 XENOLAB**: Strains, the culture bank, culture stock, the spawn planner, the species files and the hazard index.
- **4 CARGO HOLD**: Everything in stock: supplies, fresh mushrooms in the COLD STORE, and surplus or waste.
- **5 TRADE DOCK**: Selling: new sales, the sales log, buyers, standing orders, the price list, money owed, sales trends, trace and recall. Owner only.
- **6 BURSAR**: The money side: profit and loss, cash flow, the budget, what-if, cost of goods, power bills, labour and the PRICE BOOK. Owner only.
- **7 ENGINEERING**: Power, rooms, climate and upkeep: the power grid, power cuts, the deck plan, life support, water, upkeep, checklists and reminders.
- **8 MAINFRAME**: The app itself: this guide, the textbook, edit history, the data vault, crew access and tuning.

### MOVING AROUND

- **NUMBERS**: Type 1 to 8 at the > prompt to open a deck. Type 5.3 (or 5 3) to open deck 5, station 3. Type 0 for the main menu.
- **NAMES**: Type a deck name (BURSAR) or a station name (COLD STORE). The old screen names still work too: CARGO, VITALS, MISSION and the rest.
- **THE TITLE BAR**: It shows where you are: MY/CO/L // TRADE DOCK // MONEY OWED. Tap MY/CO/L for the main menu, or the deck name for its overview. On a phone it shows the screen name only.
- **THE LEFT PANEL**: On a desktop the deck and its stations head the left panel. The open station is the bright bar. Under the list, RELATED shows stations that go with this one, even on other decks.
- **ON A PHONE**: Above every station sit the deck button (back to its overview) and a drop-down of its stations. The RELATED stations are at the foot of the screen.
- **BACK**: BACK always returns to where you were, deck pages included.
- **STAFF**: Staff do not see TRADE DOCK or BURSAR, or any money figure.

### READING THE CHARTS

- **TILES**: A big figure with a label. A small line under it is the trend, and the dot is the latest value. Tap a tile to open the station it comes from.
- **COLUMNS**: One column for each day, week or month. Point at one, or touch it, and the line above the chart reads it out. A second series is hatched. A line over the columns is a third figure in the same unit.
- **BARS**: One bar for each item, all on the same scale. A short tick across a bar is a target or a limit; the words under the chart say which.
- **SPLIT BAR**: A whole split into its parts. The parts differ in brightness and hatching, and the legend under the bar gives each part with its figure.
- **DIALS**: A half circle. The shaded arc is the good band and the bright arc runs to the value.
- **AMBER**: The warning colour. Something needs you, and words beside it always say what.
- **TAP TO OPEN**: Every figure, bar and chart on a deck page opens the station behind it.

## THE PARTS OF A SCREEN

*The same frame is on every screen. Learn it once.*

### FROM TOP TO BOTTOM

- **TITLE BAR**: The dark bar at the top. It shows where you are: MY/CO/L, the deck, then the station. Tap MY/CO/L for the main menu, or the deck name for its overview. The < BACK button at the left goes back one step. On a phone the deck name is left out, and the little saving lamp sits in the status line instead.
- **STATUS LINE**: The small grey line under it: the farm clock (Costa Rica time, the same clock every log uses), DB LINKED or LOCAL MODE, NET OK or NET DOWN, the sound and look in use, the saving lamp, the ? TIPS button, the UNDO button (it shows up after your first saved change), and GLOVE when glove mode is on.
- **INDEX (LEFT)**: The menu column on the left of a desktop screen. On a station it starts with the deck and its stations, then RELATED stations, then the tabs or records of the screen you are on. On the main menu it lists every deck and station.
- **ON A PHONE**: The index is hidden. The deck button and a drop-down of its stations sit at the top of the screen, RELATED stations at the foot. Pick a tab from the SHOW list (or the strip of tabs) at the top of the page.
- **STATION LINE**: The line at the top of every station: its code, the screen name, and what the station is for.
- **MAIN PANEL**: The big box with the work in it. It scrolls by itself.
- **ANSWER BAR**: A boxed line just above the prompt that appears after you save something, undo, or type a command. It says what happened. It is red when something went wrong and stays until you close it with X; other answers fade after about 12 seconds.
- **> PROMPT**: The line with a blinking block. Type a word and press Enter: 1 to 8 opens a deck, 5.3 opens a station, a name opens a deck or a screen, FIND looks for a record. HELP lists every command.
- **HINT LINE**: One line of advice for the screen you are on, under the prompt.
- **F-KEY BAR**: The eight buttons at the bottom: F1 HELP, F2 MENU, F3 TUNE, F4 MUTE or SOUND, F5 THEME, F6 CLEAN or GLASS, F7 BOOT, F8 LOGIN or LOGOUT. On a phone you see four and a MORE key that opens the other four.

### THE SAVING LAMP AND UNDO

The lamp tells you where your last change is: SAVED HERE (on this device), SYNCED, WAITING (no signal yet) or CONFLICT. Tap it to open the saving status.

The UNDO button reverses the last saved change. It appears after you save something, and its label says what it will undo (for example UNDO ADDED HARVEST). See the SAVING page.

### COLOURS

The screen uses one colour at a time: PHOSPHOR green, CRYO blue, SODIUM amber, SPORE or ALERT red. Change it with F5 or on TUNE. Red text in any theme is a warning. CLEAN mode (F6) turns off the glass effects for bright sunlight.

## KEYS, TOUCH AND GLOVES

*Every way to move around: keyboard, mouse, finger and gloved hands.*

### KEYBOARD

- **ARROWS**: Move to the next item on the screen. Past the edge they cross to the index at the left, the BACK button at the top, or the F-keys at the bottom.
- **ENTER OR SPACE**: Press the item that has the highlight.
- **[ AND ]**: Jump to the index column, and back to the page.
- **PGUP, PGDN, HOME, END**: Scroll the page.
- **BACKSPACE**: BACK, when you are not typing in a box.
- **ESC**: Closes a pop-up or a drop-down, then leaves a box you are typing in, then goes BACK. If a tip is showing, Esc closes the tip first.
- **ALT + LEFT**: BACK from anywhere.
- **F1 TO F8**: The function bar.
- **CTRL + Z**: UNDO the last saved change (inside a typing box it stays the box's own undo).
- **ANY LETTER**: Goes to the > prompt.
- **IN A DATE BOX**: Arrows move days, PgUp and PgDn move months.
- **IN A DROP-DOWN**: Arrows, or a letter to jump, then Enter to pick.

### TOUCH (PHONE OR TABLET)

1. Tap a line, tab or button to use it. The prompt does not pop the phone keyboard up until you tap it.
2. To read what a control does, tap ? TIPS first, then tap the control. The tip has a button that opens this guide at the right page.
3. Drop-down lists open inside the terminal, never as the phone's own wheel.

### GLOVE MODE

With wet or gloved hands type GLOVE (or turn it on under TUNE). Buttons get bigger and a number pad appears for number boxes. Tap the GLOVE badge in the status line, or type GLOVE again, to turn it off.

### LANGUAGE

Type SPANISH or ESPANOL for Spanish screens, ENGLISH to come back. This guide and the tips are in English. The Spanish screen text was written by Claude and should be read by a native speaker.

## WORDS YOU CAN TYPE

*The > prompt at the bottom of every screen.*

### HOW IT WORKS

Type, press Enter. Capitals do not matter. If the app does not know the word it searches your records for it, so you can also just type a lot code like B1020 or a strain code like BO-01.

On a screen with tabs, typing a tab name (like CURVE on the cook log) switches to that tab.

### THE ONES YOU WILL USE MOST

- **MENU OR 0**: The main menu.
- **1 TO 8**: Open that deck.
- **5.3**: Open deck 5, station 3. A space or a comma works too: 5 3.
- **A DECK OR STATION NAME**: BURSAR, COLD STORE, LOG HARVEST. The old screen names still work too: CARGO, VITALS, MISSION. MONEY opens BURSAR.
- **BACK**: Go back.
- **HELP**: The full list of commands.
- **GUIDE**: This user guide, at the page for the screen you are on. GUIDE 7.1, GUIDE POWER GRID or GUIDE VITALS opens the page for that station.
- **TIPS**: Turn tip mode on or off.
- **FIND words**: Search every record and screen. Or type / and the words.
- **HARVEST, LOT, TODAY, BOARD**: Open that part of MISSION CONTROL.
- **SALE, OWED, FORECAST, PICKLIST**: Sales, money owed, harvest forecast, pick and pack list.
- **POWERCUT, WATER, CHECKLIST**: Facility logs.
- **UNDO, HISTORY, SYNC**: Undo, the change history, the saving status.
- **BACKUP, EXPORT, RESTORE**: Data safety.
- **ASK words**: Ask the farm assistant. Anything ending in ? also goes to it.
- **GLOVE, SPANISH, ENGLISH**: Glove mode and screen language.
- **THEME name, CLEAN, MUTE**: Look and sound.

## SAVING, UNDO AND HISTORY

*How the app keeps your work safe, and how to take a change back.*

### SAVING

There is no SAVE-the-whole-app button. Each form has its own save button (SAVE SALE, SAVE HARVEST and so on). The moment you press it the record is written to this device and the lamp shows SAVED HERE.

With no signal the app keeps working and queues the change. The lamp shows WAITING until it can be sent. Today, in local mode, nothing needs sending.

### UNDO

1. Press UNDO in the status line, or press Ctrl+Z, or type UNDO.
2. The last saved change is reversed. The answer bar above the prompt says what was undone.
3. You can undo many steps in a row, up to 40, until you close the app. After that use HISTORY to put old values back.

### HISTORY

Every change is written down: who made it, when, the old value and the new value. Open EDIT HISTORY (8.3). You can look at one record's whole life, or put an old value back.

### BACKUPS

Your records are only in this browser. If the browser data is wiped, or the phone is lost, they are gone. Open DATA VAULT (8.4) and use SAVE EVERYTHING (ZIP) often. (BACKUP FILE ONLY (.JSON) saves just the backup file.) The main menu warns you when the last backup is old.

## TOOLTIPS

*A short note on what any button does, on every kind of device.*

### WITH A MOUSE

Rest the pointer on a button or box for half a second. A small note appears under it. Move away and it goes.

### WITH THE KEYBOARD

Move to a control with the arrows or Tab. After a short pause the note appears. Press Esc to close it. Moving on closes it too.

### ON A PHONE OR TABLET

1. Tap ? TIPS in the status line. It lights up.
2. Tap any control. Nothing is pressed. A note says what the control does.
3. Tap OPEN THE GUIDE FOR THIS SCREEN in the note to read the full page, or CLOSE.
4. While tip mode is on, buttons do not press and nothing saves. If a button seems dead, check whether ? TIPS is lit.
5. Tap ? TIPS again, or press Esc, to go back to normal.

### GOOD TO KNOW

A few buttons have no note yet. The note then says so and offers the guide page for the screen. Rows of records such as a stock item have a short note on what tapping them does; the guide page explains the rest.

Typing TIPS at the prompt turns tip mode on and off too.

## BRIDGE OVERVIEW (1)

*Today's work and the whole farm at a glance.*

### THE OVERVIEW

## ASK MY/CO/L (1.2)

*The farm assistant: ask in plain words, or tell it something to save.*

### ASK A QUESTION

1. Type in the box, or at the > prompt: ASK words, or anything ending in ?
2. The answer comes only from your own records. It ends with a button for the screen it came from.
3. Try the EXAMPLES tab: tap one to ask it.

### SAVE BY SENTENCE

1. Type for instance: log 4.2 kg from B1020, or power out.
2. A card shows what it understood. Nothing is saved yet.
3. Press CONFIRM (or type YES) to save, or CANCEL.
4. A saved change is one UNDO step and shows in HISTORY as made by the assistant.

### THE BRAIN

Without setup a rule-based brain answers the usual sentences quickly. On the ENGINE tab you can point the assistant at a language model running on a farm computer (Ollama, llama.cpp or LM Studio). It is never a cloud service. If the model is off or slow the rules answer.

### GOOD TO KNOW

Staff get no money answers and no buyer names. Questions it cannot answer from the records get a short refusal and a line on what you can ask.

## SEARCH (1.3)

*Find any record or screen, and change many records at once.*

### FIND A RECORD

1. Type a code (B1020, BO-01) or a name in the box, or at the > prompt.
2. Results are grouped by kind. Tap one to open it.
3. A code that matches exactly opens straight away.

### CHANGE MANY AT ONCE

1. Open CHANGE MANY AT ONCE (or type MANY).
2. Choose what kind of records (lots, sales and so on) and filter to the ones you want.
3. Tick them: TICK ALL ticks every match.
4. Choose the one change (status, place, grade, paid or owed, delete).
5. Check the preview, then apply. It is one UNDO step.

### STOCK TAKE

Type STOCKTAKE to count many stock items and save once.

## GROW DECK OVERVIEW (2)

*Block lots from the steam cook to the harvest.*

### THE OVERVIEW

- **LOTS GROWING**: Lots in the rooms now, and how many blocks they hold.
- **LOST, 90 DAYS**: Blocks lost out of the blocks knocked in the last 90 days. Amber at 5% or more.
- **BE%, LAST FINISHED LOTS**: The average biological efficiency of the last 5 lots that finished.
- **BLOCKS KNOCKED IN THE LAST 90 DAYS**: Where those blocks are now: incubating, fruiting, spent or lost. A lot marked lost counts all its blocks.
- **KG PICKED A WEEK**: The last 12 weeks. The PLAN line is the crop plan's blocks a week times Catawba's kilos per block, at full production.
- **NEXT 7 DAYS**: Lots to move to fruiting and lots due for their first pick.

### STATIONS

- **2.1 LOT BOARD**: Every active lot: where it is and what is due. Bulk changes, scanning and the guesses are here too.
- **2.2 NEW BLOCK LOT**: Log the blocks you just inoculated.
- **2.3 LOG HARVEST**: Kilos picked, by lot and flush.
- **2.4 STEAM COOKS**: The steam and cook log, with temperature curves and a verdict for each run.
- **2.5 RUN PLANNER**: This week's bag run and what it needs.
- **2.6 YIELD RECORDS**: BE% by lot and flush, the scorecard and strain trials.

## MISSION CONTROL (1.1, 2.1 TO 2.3)

*Every block lot on the farm, today's jobs, and the harvest log.*

### ITS STATIONS

Four stations open this screen. Each one shows only its own tabs.

- **1.1 TODAY'S WORK**: The TODAY tab, on the BRIDGE.
- **2.1 LOT BOARD**: The BOARD tab, with BULK ACTIONS, SCAN A LABEL and GUESSES.
- **2.2 NEW BLOCK LOT**: The NEW BLOCK LOT tab.
- **2.3 LOG HARVEST**: The LOG HARVEST tab.

### WORDS TO KNOW

- **BLOCK**: One bag of substrate with spawn in it. It grows mushrooms.
- **LOT**: A batch of blocks knocked (inoculated) on the same day. A lot has a code like B1020: a letter for the species, then the month and day.
- **FLUSH**: One wave of mushrooms from a block. Most species give one or two.
- **STATUS**: Where the lot is: incubating, fruiting, or spent.

### THE TABS

- **BOARD**: Every active lot with its days, blocks, picks and what to do next.
- **TODAY**: Jobs for today: lots to move to fruiting, lots to pick, orders short of stock, strains slipping.
- **LOG HARVEST**: Record a pick.
- **NEW BLOCK LOT**: Record a new lot right after the knock.
- **BULK ACTIONS**: Harvest session, inoculate a cart, change many lots, split a lot.
- **SCAN A LABEL**: Read a QR label with the camera, or type the code.
- **GUESSES**: Numbers the app guessed (move window, number of flushes) for you to check.

### LOG A HARVEST

1. Open LOG HARVEST (2.3).
2. Pick the lot (or type its code).
3. Type the weight in kilograms. The grade starts on A: change it to B or SECONDS if needed. Type the flush number (1 for the first pick of a block).
4. Add your initials.
5. Press SAVE HARVEST. The pick shows under RECENT PICKS and feeds YIELD RECORDS, the COLD STORE and COST OF GOODS.
6. Made a mistake? Press UNDO straight away.

### START A NEW LOT

1. Open NEW BLOCK LOT (2.2) after you have inoculated the blocks.
2. Pick the species and strain, the knock date and how many blocks.
3. Press LOG BLOCK LOT. The app makes the code. It starts as incubating.

### GOOD TO KNOW

A lot moves to fruiting by itself when you log its first harvest. If a code is already used on that day the app adds -2.

Staff can use all of this screen.

## STEAM COOKS (2.4)

*The steam and sterilization log with temperature curves and a verdict. The screen is called PROPULSION.*

### LOG A RUN

1. Press a start button: STEAM BOX RUN, 75X RUN, MEGACHEF RUN or OTHER RUN.
2. Pick the date and what was cooked, and how many bags.
3. Add temperature readings as you go (QUICK-ADD on a phone), or paste the file from a data logger.
4. Open CURVE to see the temperature against the targets and the verdict (pass, warn or fail).

### THE TABS

- **RUN**: Start a run, or pick one from the list.
- **CURVE**: The temperature curve, time at temperature and the verdict.
- **READINGS**: The readings table and a form to add more.
- **SHEET**: A log sheet to print and fill in on paper.
- **TARGETS**: The targets and where they came from.

### GOOD TO KNOW

Targets marked GUESS - REVIEW are the app's guesses. Check them against your own procedure.

CURVE, READINGS and SHEET show one run. With no run picked they say so and offer the latest run.

## RUN PLANNER (2.5)

*The planner for this week's bag run. The screen is called PRODUCTION SEQUENCE.*

### WHAT IT DOES

You say how many bags and which species. The planner shows how the steam box cart fills, how many bags each species gets, and what stock the run uses.

### MAKE A PLAN

1. Type the number of bags under BAGS THIS RUN, or tap 130 STANDARD.
2. Pick SINGLE (one species) or MULTI (a mix) under SUGGESTED RUNS.
3. For a mix, tick the species under SPECIES IN THIS RUN. The ALLOTMENT list shows each share.
4. The second slot is a small fixed run (16 bags a week) of one extra species of your pick.
5. Press INITIATE PRODUCTION SEQUENCE to run the animation of the plan (optional).

## COLD STORE (4.2)

*Fresh mushrooms on hand: picked, minus sold fresh, minus surplus and waste.*

### WHAT YOU SEE

- **ON HAND NOW**: Fresh kilos still in the cold store, all species together.
- **OLDEST PICK**: The age of the oldest pick still on hand. It turns amber two days before the shelf life runs out: sell, dry or donate it first. Tap it to change the shelf life.
- **PICKED, SOLD FRESH**: Kilos picked, and kilos sold fresh, in the last 7 days.
- **STALE, NOT LOGGED**: Kilos that went past the shelf life in the last 30 days and were never logged as sold or written off. It shows only when there are some. Tap it to log where they went.
- **BY SPECIES**: A bar for each species on hand, then a table of the last 7 days: picked, sold fresh, written off, on hand, oldest and stale.
- **PICKS STILL ON HAND**: Each day's pick that is not yet sold, oldest first.

### HOW IT COUNTS

Nothing is typed in here. The store is worked out from three logs: LOG HARVEST adds kilos, a fresh sale in NEW SALE takes them off, and SURPLUS + WASTE takes them off too.

A fresh sale takes kilos off the oldest picks of its species that were still fresh on the day of the sale. Surplus or waste also takes the oldest picks first, and can clear picks that went off.

A pick older than the shelf life no longer counts as on hand. If it was never logged as sold or waste, it shows as stale for 30 days.

Dried sales do not count here: drying is logged as surplus.

### GOOD TO KNOW

The shelf life is a GUESS: 7 days for every species. Change it in the PRICE BOOK (6.8).

Owner only, because it reads the sales.

The alarm limits and delays are GUESSES until you confirm them.

## EDIT HISTORY (8.3)

### THE PAGES

## DATA VAULT (8.4)

*Backups, spreadsheets, restoring and the Google Sheet. The screen is called DATA.*

### MAKE A BACKUP

1. Open BACK UP.
2. Press SAVE EVERYTHING (ZIP). A file downloads: the backup, a README and one spreadsheet for every table that has records. (BACKUP FILE ONLY (.JSON) downloads just the backup file.)
3. Move the file somewhere safe: a USB stick, your e-mail, another computer.
4. Do it every week at least. The main menu warns you when it is old.

### OTHER PAGES

- **EXPORT SPREADSHEETS**: One table as a CSV file you can open in a spreadsheet.
- **RESTORE**: Put a backup back.
- **GOOGLE SHEET**: Every record mirrored into one Google Sheet. Edits typed in the sheet come back into the app.
- **GUESSES**: The stale-backup rule and other guessed numbers.

### RESTORE A BACKUP

1. Open RESTORE and pick the backup file.
2. The app shows what would change: how many records are added, changed or removed.
3. Choose ADD AND UPDATE (KEEP THE REST) or the exact restore. A RESTORE button appears with the number of changes.
4. Press it. It asks SURE? TAP AGAIN: press it a second time to really restore.
5. Everything restored is one UNDO step.

### LINK THE GOOGLE SHEET (ONCE)

1. Open GOOGLE SHEET and press SHEETS.NEW to make an empty Google Sheet.
2. In the sheet open Extensions > Apps Script. Delete what is there. Press COPY SCRIPT in the app and paste it in. Press Save.
3. Press Deploy > New deployment > Web app. Execute as: Me. Who has access: Anyone. Press Deploy, then Allow.
4. Copy the Web app URL (it ends in /exec), paste it into SHEET ADDRESS and press SAVE.
5. Press SYNC NOW. The first sync makes a READ ME tab and one tab per record type.

### EDIT IN THE SHEET

1. Change any white cell. The app picks it up within 5 minutes, or press SYNC NOW.
2. Add a record by typing in the first empty row. Type a strain code or lot code in the look-up column and the app finds the record.
3. Delete a record by typing DELETE in its _action cell. Deleting the whole row does not delete it: the app puts it back.
4. Each sync is one UNDO step, signed GOOGLE SHEET in HISTORY.

### GOOD TO KNOW

Restoring needs the file from the same app. A file from anywhere else is refused. Owner only.

In the sheet, grey cells and grey tabs (exchange rates, edit history, sensor readings) are read only. A value that does not fit is refused and listed under LISTED BY THE SYNC; the app value goes back into the sheet.

## CREW ACCESS (8.5)

*Who is using this device and what they may open. The screen is called STAFF.*

### THE ROLES

- **OWNER**: Sees everything.
- **STAFF**: Logs the work and sees no money, history, data or textbook. TRADE DOCK, BURSAR and the COLD STORE are hidden.

### LOCK A DEVICE TO STAFF

1. Type the OWNER PIN (4 to 12 digits) twice and press SAVE PIN.
2. Press LOCK THIS DEVICE TO STAFF. It asks SURE? TAP AGAIN: press it a second time.
3. To open the money screens again type the owner PIN on this screen.

### GOOD TO KNOW

In local mode this lock keeps a helper out of the money screens but is not real security, because the data sits in this browser. Real separation needs the database login. Five wrong PIN tries wait 30 seconds.

## TUNING (8.6, F3)

*The look, the sound, language and gloves, saved on this device.*

### SECTIONS

- **LOOK**: Theme colour, BRIGHTNESS for the whole screen, WIREFRAMES for how bright the species models draw, glass strength (curve, grain, scanlines, glow), CLEAN mode for sunlight.
- **SOUND**: Sound on or off, the typing sound, chatter or tick mode, volume. TEST buttons play each sound so you can hear it. Sound only plays when something appears on screen.
- **LANGUAGE AND GLOVES**: English or Spanish, and glove mode.
- **SYSTEM**: Replay the boot, test the alarm.

### GOOD TO KNOW

These settings belong to this device only. A phone and a laptop each keep their own. They are not part of UNDO.

To drag a meter with the keyboard, focus it and use the arrow keys.

Nothing here is ever greyed out. Moving a glass slider while CLEAN mode is on brings the glass back. Moving a sound slider or pressing a TEST button while sound is muted turns sound back on.

## WORDS AND SHORT FORMS

*What the farm words and codes mean.*

### THE LIST

- **BE%**: Fresh kg picked divided by dry kg of substrate, times 100.
- **BLOCK**: One bag of substrate with spawn.
- **CART**: A rack of blocks that moves together. It has a name like C-07.
- **COGS**: Cost of goods sold: what the mushrooms cost to make.
- **FLUSH**: One wave of mushrooms from a block.
- **G1, G2**: First and second grain spawn generations.
- **ICE**: Costa Rica's electricity utility. Its bill has a charge for energy and one for the highest demand.
- **KNOCK**: The day blocks are inoculated.
- **LOT**: Blocks knocked on the same day. Code like B1020.
- **P0, P1, P2**: Steps away from the stored master culture.
- **RH**: Relative humidity, in percent.
- **SLANT, WATER TUBE**: Ways to store a culture for months.
- **T/E TEST**: Test and expand: grow a test batch before using a stored culture in production.
- **75X**: The small sterilizer used in the lab.

## IF SOMETHING GOES WRONG

*Common problems and what to try.*

### MY CHANGE IS WRONG

Press UNDO right away. If it was a while ago, open HISTORY and put the old value back.

### THE DATA LOOKS EMPTY ON A DIFFERENT DEVICE

In local mode each device keeps its own records. Make a backup on the device that has them (DATA VAULT, 8.4) and restore it on the other one.

### A BUTTON DOES NOTHING WHEN I TAP IT

Tip mode may be on. The ? TIPS button in the status line is lit. Tap it to turn tip mode off.

### I CANNOT OPEN A SCREEN

The answer bar above the prompt says why. A staff login cannot open money screens, history, data or the textbook. Ask the owner.

### THE SCREEN IS HARD TO READ

Press F6 for CLEAN mode in sunlight, or F5 to change the colour, or open TUNING (F3) and raise BRIGHTNESS or lower the glass strength. WIREFRAMES on the same screen brightens just the species models.

### THERE IS NO SOUND

Sound plays only when text appears, and phones only allow it after a tap. Check F4 shows MUTE (sound is on) and that the phone is not on silent.

### THE LAMP SAYS WAITING OR CONFLICT

WAITING means changes are saved on this device and still to be sent. CONFLICT means two devices changed the same record: open HISTORY, then SAVING STATUS, to see what was kept.
