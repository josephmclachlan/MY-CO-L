## START HERE

*What MY/CO/L is, and your first five minutes with it.*

### WHAT THIS IS

MY/CO/L is the farm terminal for Talamanca Station. It keeps the farm records in one place: the crops, the cultures, the stock, the sales, the power, the room climate and the cash.

It looks like an old green-screen computer on purpose. Everything on it works by tapping or clicking, by keyboard, or by typing short words at the > prompt at the bottom of the screen.

### YOUR FIRST FIVE MINUTES

1. Tap or press any key on the dark start screen. The boot text runs, then the MAIN MENU appears. Any key or tap skips the boot text (unless TUNE > BOOT is set to ALWAYS FULL).
2. On the MAIN MENU, tap a module (a numbered line) to open it. You can also type its number or name at the > prompt and press Enter. Try 12 for MISSION CONTROL.
3. Look at the top left of any screen for the BACK button. It returns you to the screen you came from, with everything as you left it.
4. Tap ? TIPS in the grey line under the title bar. Now tap a button: instead of pressing it, you get a note saying what it does. Tap ? TIPS again to leave. While tip mode is on, buttons do not press, so nothing saves.
5. Open this guide from the MAIN MENU (module 23) or type GUIDE. On any screen, GUIDE opens the page for that screen.

### WHERE YOUR RECORDS LIVE

Today the app runs in LOCAL MODE. Your records are saved inside this browser on this device. Nothing is sent anywhere. A phone and a laptop each have their own copy until a database is linked later.

Because of that, make a backup often. See the DATA page. A backup is one file you can keep on a USB stick or send to yourself.

The app works with no internet and no signal. When there is no network the status line says NET DOWN // CACHED, and you can keep working.

### OWNER AND STAFF

The OWNER sees everything. A STAFF login logs the work (harvests, lots, power cuts, readings) and never sees money. Staff do not see pages in this guide for screens they cannot open.

## THE PARTS OF A SCREEN

*The same frame is on every screen. Learn it once.*

### FROM TOP TO BOTTOM

- **TITLE BAR**: The dark bar at the top. It shows the screen name. The < BACK button at the left goes back one step. On a phone the little saving lamp sits in the status line instead.
- **STATUS LINE**: The small grey line under it: the farm clock (Costa Rica time, the same clock every log uses), DB LINKED or LOCAL MODE, NET OK or NET DOWN, the sound and look in use, the saving lamp, the ? TIPS button, the UNDO button (it shows up after your first saved change), and GLOVE when glove mode is on.
- **INDEX (LEFT)**: The menu column on the left of a desktop screen. It lists the tabs or records for the screen you are on. On a phone it is hidden: pick the tab from the SHOW list (or the strip of tabs) at the top of the page instead.
- **MAIN PANEL**: The big box with the work in it. It scrolls by itself.
- **ANSWER BAR**: A boxed line just above the prompt that appears after you save something, undo, or type a command. It says what happened. It is red when something went wrong and stays until you close it with X; other answers fade after about 12 seconds.
- **> PROMPT**: The line with a blinking block. Type a word and press Enter: a number opens a module, a name opens a screen, FIND looks for a record. HELP lists every command.
- **HINT LINE**: One line of advice for the screen you are on, under the prompt.
- **F-KEY BAR**: The eight buttons at the bottom: F1 HELP, F2 MENU, F3 TUNE, F4 MUTE or SOUND, F5 THEME, F6 CLEAN or GLASS, F7 BOOT, F8 LOGIN or LOGOUT. On a phone you see four and a MORE key that opens the other four.

### THE SAVING LAMP AND UNDO

The lamp tells you where your last change is: SAVED HERE (on this device), SYNCED, WAITING (no signal yet) or CONFLICT. Tap it to open the saving status.

The UNDO button reverses the last saved change. It appears after you save something, and its label says what it will undo (for example UNDO ADDED HARVEST). See the SAVING page.

### COLOURS

The screen uses one colour at a time: PHOSPHOR green, CRYO blue, SODIUM amber, SPORE or ALERT red. Change it with F5 or on TUNE. Red text in any theme is a warning. CLEAN mode (F6) turns off the glass effects for bright sunlight.

## KEYS, TOUCH AND GLOVES

*Every way to move around: keyboard, mouse, finger and gloved hands.*

### KEYBOARD

- **ARROWS**: Move to the next item on the screen. Past the edge they cross to the index at the left, the BACK button at the top, or the F-keys at the bottom.
- **ENTER OR SPACE**: Press the item that has the highlight.
- **[ AND ]**: Jump to the index column, and back to the page.
- **PGUP, PGDN, HOME, END**: Scroll the page.
- **BACKSPACE**: BACK, when you are not typing in a box.
- **ESC**: Closes a pop-up or a drop-down, then leaves a box you are typing in, then goes BACK. If a tip is showing, Esc closes the tip first.
- **ALT + LEFT**: BACK from anywhere.
- **F1 TO F8**: The function bar.
- **CTRL + Z**: UNDO the last saved change (inside a typing box it stays the box's own undo).
- **ANY LETTER**: Goes to the > prompt.
- **IN A DATE BOX**: Arrows move days, PgUp and PgDn move months.
- **IN A DROP-DOWN**: Arrows, or a letter to jump, then Enter to pick.

### TOUCH (PHONE OR TABLET)

1. Tap a line, tab or button to use it. The prompt does not pop the phone keyboard up until you tap it.
2. To read what a control does, tap ? TIPS first, then tap the control. The tip has a button that opens this guide at the right page.
3. Drop-down lists open inside the terminal, never as the phone's own wheel.

### GLOVE MODE

With wet or gloved hands type GLOVE (or turn it on under TUNE). Buttons get bigger and a number pad appears for number boxes. Tap the GLOVE badge in the status line, or type GLOVE again, to turn it off.

### LANGUAGE

Type SPANISH or ESPANOL for Spanish screens, ENGLISH to come back. This guide and the tips are in English. The Spanish screen text was written by Claude and should be read by a native speaker.

On a screen with tabs, typing a tab name (like CURVE on the cook log) switches to that tab.

- **HARVEST, LOT, TODAY, BOARD**: Open that part of MISSION CONTROL.

- **POWERCUT, WATER, CHECKLIST**: Facility logs.
- **UNDO, HISTORY, SYNC**: Undo, the change history, the saving status.
- **BACKUP, EXPORT, RESTORE**: Data safety.

- **GLOVE, SPANISH, ENGLISH**: Glove mode and screen language.
- **THEME name, CLEAN, MUTE**: Look and sound.

## SAVING, UNDO AND HISTORY

*How the app keeps your work safe, and how to take a change back.*

### SAVING

There is no SAVE-the-whole-app button. Each form has its own save button (SAVE SALE, SAVE HARVEST and so on). The moment you press it the record is written to this device and the lamp shows SAVED HERE.

With no signal the app keeps working and queues the change. The lamp shows WAITING until it can be sent. Today, in local mode, nothing needs sending.

### UNDO

1. Press UNDO in the status line, or press Ctrl+Z, or type UNDO.
2. The last saved change is reversed. The answer bar above the prompt says what was undone.
3. You can undo many steps in a row, up to 40, until you close the app. After that use HISTORY to put old values back.

### HISTORY

Every change is written down: who made it, when, the old value and the new value. Open HISTORY (module 13). You can look at one record's whole life, or put an old value back.

### BACKUPS

Your records are only in this browser. If the browser data is wiped, or the phone is lost, they are gone. Open DATA and use SAVE EVERYTHING (ZIP) often. (BACKUP FILE ONLY (.JSON) saves just the backup file.) The main menu warns you when the last backup is old.

## TOOLTIPS

*A short note on what any button does, on every kind of device.*

### WITH A MOUSE

Rest the pointer on a button or box for half a second. A small note appears under it. Move away and it goes.

### WITH THE KEYBOARD

Move to a control with the arrows or Tab. After a short pause the note appears. Press Esc to close it. Moving on closes it too.

### ON A PHONE OR TABLET

1. Tap ? TIPS in the status line. It lights up.
2. Tap any control. Nothing is pressed. A note says what the control does.
3. Tap OPEN THE GUIDE FOR THIS SCREEN in the note to read the full page, or CLOSE.
4. While tip mode is on, buttons do not press and nothing saves. If a button seems dead, check whether ? TIPS is lit.
5. Tap ? TIPS again, or press Esc, to go back to normal.

### GOOD TO KNOW

A few buttons have no note yet. The note then says so and offers the guide page for the screen. Rows of records such as a stock item have a short note on what tapping them does; the guide page explains the rest.

Typing TIPS at the prompt turns tip mode on and off too.

## MISSION CONTROL (12)

*Every block lot on the farm, today's jobs, and the harvest log.*

### WORDS TO KNOW

- **BLOCK**: One bag of substrate with spawn in it. It grows mushrooms.
- **LOT**: A batch of blocks knocked (inoculated) on the same day. A lot has a code like B1020: a letter for the species, then the month and day.
- **FLUSH**: One wave of mushrooms from a block. Most species give one or two.
- **STATUS**: Where the lot is: incubating, fruiting, or spent.

### THE TABS

- **BOARD**: Every active lot with its days, blocks, picks and what to do next.
- **TODAY**: Jobs for today: lots to move to fruiting, lots to pick, orders short of stock, strains slipping.
- **LOG HARVEST**: Record a pick.
- **NEW BLOCK LOT**: Record a new lot right after the knock.
- **BULK ACTIONS**: Harvest session, inoculate a cart, change many lots, split a lot.
- **SCAN A LABEL**: Read a QR label with the camera, or type the code.
- **GUESSES**: Numbers the app guessed (move window, number of flushes) for you to check.

### LOG A HARVEST

1. Open LOG HARVEST.
2. Pick the lot (or type its code).
3. Type the weight in kilograms. The grade starts on A: change it to B or SECONDS if needed. Type the flush number (1 for the first pick of a block).
4. Add your initials.
5. Press SAVE HARVEST. The pick shows under RECENT PICKS and feeds PRODUCTION RECORDS, LEDGER and TRADE.
6. Made a mistake? Press UNDO straight away.

### START A NEW LOT

1. Open NEW BLOCK LOT after you have inoculated the blocks.
2. Pick the species and strain, the knock date and how many blocks.
3. Press LOG BLOCK LOT. The app makes the code. It starts as incubating.

### GOOD TO KNOW

A lot moves to fruiting by itself when you log its first harvest. If a code is already used on that day the app adds -2.

Staff can use all of this screen.

## PRODUCTION RECORDS (14)

*How well each lot, strain and room is doing, and strain trials.*

### WORDS TO KNOW

- **BE%**: Biological efficiency: fresh kilograms picked divided by the dry kilograms of substrate, times 100. A way to compare lots fairly. One 10 lb block holds 4 lb of dry substrate.
- **FLUSH LOG**: One line per lot and flush.
- **FADING STRAIN**: A strain whose newest lots yield clearly less than its earlier ones. It may be tired and need replacing from the bank.

### THE TABS

- **FLUSH LOG**: BE% for each lot and flush, with the day the first pins appeared.
- **SCORECARD**: Results grouped by strain, cook run, room, week or worker. Pick a grouping at the top.
- **STRAIN TRIALS**: Put two or more strains side by side and see which wins. START TRIAL begins one.
- **GUESSES**: The rules the app guessed, for you to check.

### GOOD TO KNOW

Only complete lots (spent, or all flushes picked) count toward averages. Fading needs at least five finished lots of a strain.

## CARGO (4)

*The stock list: what you have, what is low, what to reorder.*

### HOW STOCK WORKS

There is no typed-in count. What you have is the sum of every move: deliveries add, use subtracts, a count adds or subtracts the difference. That way the history of every item is kept.

### THE LIST

- **REORDER**: Items at or under their reorder point, with how many weeks of stock are left.
- **CATEGORY TABS**: SUBSTRATE, GRAIN AND SPAWN, CULTURE LAB, CLEANING AND PPE, AIR AND WATER.
- **ADD ITEM**: Add a new item: name, unit and category (OTHER if none fits). Set its reorder point and lead time afterwards on its own page.
- **STOCK TAKE**: Count a shelf and save all the new numbers at once.
- **EXPORT BACKUP, IMPORT BACKUP**: Save or load the whole stock list as a file.
- **RESTORE STARTER LIST**: Put the starter items back.

### RECEIVE, USE OR COUNT AN ITEM

1. Tap the item in the list. Its page opens.
2. Use the block you need: RECEIVE DELIVERY (quantity, price per unit, supplier, then BOOK DELIVERY; PRICE ONLY records a price without changing the count), RECORD USE (BOOK USE) or SET COUNT (SAVE COUNT).
3. The MOVES list shows what you saved, and UNDO reverses it.
4. Reorder point and lead time are on the same page under ABOUT THIS ITEM.

### GOOD TO KNOW

Weekly use comes from the production plan (130 bags a week). The substrate mix (60:40, 55:45 or 50:50) changes how much sawdust and soy hulls it needs. Prices are hidden from staff.

## PRODUCTION SEQUENCE (10)

*The planner for this week's bag run.*

### WHAT IT DOES

You say how many bags and which species. The planner shows how the steam box cart fills, how many bags each species gets, and what stock the run uses.

### MAKE A PLAN

1. Type the number of bags under BAGS THIS RUN, or tap 130 STANDARD.
2. Pick SINGLE (one species) or MULTI (a mix) under SUGGESTED RUNS.
3. For a mix, tick the species under SPECIES IN THIS RUN. The ALLOTMENT list shows each share.
4. The second slot is a small fixed run (16 bags a week) of one extra species of your pick.
5. Press INITIATE PRODUCTION SEQUENCE to run the animation of the plan (optional).
6. Press COMMIT RUN TO CARGO to book the stock out of CARGO. CARGO lists the run and can undo it. If CARGO has no counts yet the stock will show as negative: count it first with STOCK TAKE.

## FACILITY LOGS (17)

*Power cuts, water tests, upkeep, cleaning, checklists and reminders.*

### THE TABS

- **STATUS**: A summary: is the power on, when was water last tested, what is due.
- **POWER CUTS**: The log of cuts. See below.
- **WATER**: Water test results against their limits.
- **CHECKLISTS**: Daily or weekly checklists to sign off.
- **MAINTENANCE**: The upkeep jobs with their intervals and what is overdue.
- **CLEANING**: The cleaning log with targets per room.
- **REMINDERS**: Everything due now or soon. You can snooze or download a phone calendar file.

### WHEN THE POWER GOES OUT

1. The moment the grid fails press POWER WENT OUT NOW. Press it on the VITALS screen too: both do the same.
2. If you start the generator press GENERATOR STARTED NOW. Press GENERATOR STOPPED NOW when it stops. (Before a cut the screen offers GENERATOR TEST STARTED NOW instead, to log a test run.)
3. When the grid comes back press POWER IS BACK NOW.
4. Add fuel used, what was affected, and any losses.
5. If you forgot to press the button, use LOG A PAST CUT and type the start and end times.
6. The log counts hours with no grid, hours on the generator and hours fully dark for each month.

### GOOD TO KNOW

All times use the farm clock (Costa Rica, UTC-6), the same clock shown in the status line.

A cut over 30 days cannot be closed with the button: use EDIT.

Limits and intervals are GUESSES until you check them. See the GUESSES tab.

## SPECIMEN FILES (1)

*One file per species: how to grow it, with a turning wireframe model.*

### HOW TO USE IT

1. Tap a species in the list (on a phone use the strip at the top).
2. Read the tabs: OVERVIEW, CULTIVATION, TIMELINE, CLIMATE, YIELD, HAZARDS, NOTES, SOURCES.
3. Tap WHY? beside a number to see where it came from.

## XENOGENETICS (5)

*Strains, cultures, the culture bank, lineage, the planner and labels.*

### WORDS TO KNOW

- **MASTER (P0)**: The stored original. It arrived as a slant, a water tube or a plate.
- **P-LEVEL**: How many steps from the master. A plate made from the master is P1. A plate made from that plate is P2. Slants and tubes carry the level of what they were made from.
- **G1, G2**: Grain spawn generations: G1 (master grain) is made from a culture, G2 from G1. G2 goes into blocks.
- **SLANT, WATER TUBE**: The two ways cultures are stored long term here. There is no freezer.
- **STRAIN CODE**: Two letters for the species and a number: BO-01 blue oyster, LM-01 lion's mane.

### THE TABS

- **STRAINS**: Every strain you hold. REGISTER adds one.
- **BANK**: The culture bank: slants and water tubes, due dates, viability checks, T/E tests.
- **STOCK**: Cultures and grain runs in stock.
- **LINEAGE**: A strain's family tree. Tap a node to log the next step.
- **PLANNER**: Works back from the weekly bag run to how many G2, G1 and masters you need.
- **LABELS**: QR labels to print and stick on dishes, bags and tubes.

### ADD A STRAIN

1. Open STRAINS.
2. Pick the species, type the strain code, name and source.
3. Say what it arrived as (the master is P0).
4. Press REGISTER.

### BANK RULES THE APP FOLLOWS

Only bank from a P1 or later plate, never from the P0 master. Slants are replaced after about six months and water tubes are checked on the same schedule. A tube over two years old is suspect until a test passes. Dates and warnings show on the BANK tab.

## HAZARD INDEX (6)

*Contaminants, pests and faults: how to spot, cause, treat and prevent.*

### FIND ONE

1. Browse the list at the left, or filter by kind (MOULD, BACTERIA, and so on) or by where you saw it (PLATE, GRAIN SPAWN, COOK + COOL-DOWN).
2. Or type a name at the > prompt (try BLOTCH).
3. Or press DIAGNOSE and describe the colour, smell and place. It lists the likely matches.

### ONE ENTRY

- **SPOT**: How to recognise it.
- **LOOK-ALIKES**: What it is often mistaken for.
- **CAUSE**: What causes it.
- **TREAT**: What to do.
- **PREVENT**: How to stop it.
- **SPECIES**: The species it affects.
- **LOSSES**: Losses you logged against it.
- **SOURCES**: Where the text came from.

### GOOD TO KNOW

Some library text is only in the owner's private copy of the app, not in the public one. The screen still works without it.

## PROPULSION: COOK LOG (7)

*The steam and sterilization log with temperature curves and a verdict.*

### LOG A RUN

1. Press a start button: STEAM BOX RUN, 75X RUN, MEGACHEF RUN or OTHER RUN.
2. Pick the date and what was cooked, and how many bags.
3. Add temperature readings as you go (QUICK-ADD on a phone), or paste the file from a data logger.
4. Open CURVE to see the temperature against the targets and the verdict (pass, warn or fail).

### THE TABS

- **RUN**: Start a run, or pick one from the list.
- **CURVE**: The temperature curve, time at temperature and the verdict.
- **READINGS**: The readings table and a form to add more.
- **SHEET**: A log sheet to print and fill in on paper.
- **TARGETS**: The targets and where they came from.

### GOOD TO KNOW

Targets marked GUESS - REVIEW are the app's guesses. Check them against your own procedure.

## VITALS (2)

*The power board: breakers against what the supply can carry.*

### HOW TO USE IT

1. Choose the source: ICE UTILITY (the grid) or GENERATOR.
2. Choose the steam heater: A (two 4,500 W elements) or B (one 6,500 W element).
3. Tap a breaker to flip it on or off. Or type its tag at the prompt (S4, 13).
4. Watch the budget bar. It turns to warning colour as you near the limit.
5. Use a PRESET for a quick set: BASELINE, EVERYTHING ON, ALL OFF and others.

### THE SECTIONS

- **BUDGET**: Amps and watts on, against the 200 A service or the generator.
- **POWER CUTS**: Buttons for a cut in progress. Same as FACILITY LOGS.
- **RULES**: Things the board checks, like linked breakers.
- **PRESETS**: One-tap sets of breakers.
- **MAIN PANEL, SUB-PANEL**: The two panels with every breaker.
- **DETAIL**: The breaker you picked: its load and room.

### GOOD TO KNOW

This board is a planning tool. Flipping a breaker here does not switch real power. Some room placements are guesses until confirmed.

## DECK PLAN (3)

*The floor plan with the rooms, their equipment and set points.*

### HOW TO USE IT

1. Tap a room on the plan, or in the list.
2. Under the plan you see that room's SET POINTS, EQUIPMENT, CIRCUITS and INVENTORY.
3. OPEN VITALS or OPEN CARGO jump to the power board or the stock list.

### GOOD TO KNOW

Each room also shows its live climate and any alarm (from ATMOSPHERICS). The plan is drawn from the real floor plan. Room names and some equipment placements are guesses until confirmed.

## ATMOSPHERICS (18)

*Room temperature, humidity and CO2: readings, graphs and alarms.*

### THE TABS

- **NOW**: A card for each room with its latest reading. Tap a card for its graph.
- **GRAPHS**: Graphs over the last day, week or month with the allowed band shaded.
- **ALARMS**: Alarms raised, with a message you can copy for WhatsApp.
- **LOG READING**: Type in a reading from a hand meter.
- **IMPORT CSV**: Bring in a file exported from a Govee app.
- **AC UNITS**: Log what each air conditioner is doing, and any error code.
- **SET POINTS**: The limits for each room. Tap EDIT on a room to change them.
- **SENSORS**: The list of sensors.
- **FEEDS**: What was found out about reading Govee and Lennox automatically.
- **MOCK TEST**: Made-up readings to see how alarms and graphs behave.

### LOG A HAND READING

1. Open LOG READING.
2. Pick the room: check which one is highlighted, because one is already chosen for you (FRUITING).
3. Type temperature, humidity or CO2 (you may leave some blank). The band for the room is shown beside the box.
4. Press SAVE READING.

### GOOD TO KNOW

Full automatic sensor hookup waits until the farm is built and a bridge computer is set up. For now readings come from you, a CSV file or the mock test.

The alarm limits and delays are GUESSES until you confirm them.

## LEDGER (8)

*What a block costs, with electricity bills, hours and every price in one place.*

### THE PAGES

- **OVERVIEW + PLAN WEEK**: Cost of goods: what one block and one kilogram cost, split into parts, and the cost of the planned week.
- **ICE BILLS**: Type in each electricity bill. The app checks the bill against the ICE tariff.
- **LABOUR HOURS**: Log hours worked. They count toward cost when labour is switched on.
- **PRICES + COSTS**: Every price and cost the app uses: sale price per kg, electricity tariff, labour rate, wear. Blank means the built-in number.

### TYPE AN ELECTRICITY BILL

1. Open ICE BILLS.
2. Type the month, kWh used, billed kW (demand) and the total in colones.
3. Press SAVE BILL. Room energy in the cost uses real bills for the months you typed.

### GOOD TO KNOW

Money is stored in colones only. US dollars are worked out at the rate saved for each record's own day, so old figures never change when the rate does.

Owner only.

## TRADE (9)

*Record sales and see totals and margin.*

### RECORD A SALE

1. Open NEW SALE.
2. Pick or type the BUYER, the channel (MARKET, RESTAURANT, SHOP, DIRECT, DELIVERY, OTHER), the species and FRESH or DRIED.
3. Type the weight in KG and the grade. The price per kg box may be empty and show BUILT-IN with a price: leave it for that price, or type your own if this sale differs. Set the built-in prices in PRICES + COSTS.
4. Optionally type the lot codes (B1020 L1022). They are what lets TRACE find who got a lot.
5. Choose PAID ON DELIVERY or NOT PAID YET.
6. Press SAVE SALE. UNDO reverses it.

### THE TABS

- **NEW SALE**: The form above.
- **TOTALS + MARGIN**: Sales by species and channel and the margin against cost.
- **BUYERS**: Every buyer with what they bought and owe.
- **SALES LOG**: Every sale, newest first.
- **GUESSES**: Things the app guessed.

### GOOD TO KNOW

Every buyer pays cash on delivery. A sale marked NOT PAID YET goes onto MONEY and puts that buyer on hold: the app warns you on the next sale and on the pick list, it does not block.

## STANDING ORDERS (15)

*Regular weekly orders, the harvest forecast, the pick list and the WhatsApp price list.*

### THE TABS

- **STANDING ORDERS**: Each buyer's regular order: kg a week, price, delivery day. A buyer who would take more than the cap of the crop (30%) is flagged.
- **HARVEST FORECAST**: What each lot should give and when, week by week, against the orders.
- **PICK + PACK LIST**: For a delivery day: what to pick and pack for each buyer, and whether there is enough. COPY PICK SHEET copies it as text.
- **PRICE LIST (WHATSAPP)**: What is available this week and at what price, as a message. OPEN WHATSAPP sends it from your phone.
- **GUESSES**: The rules the app guessed: the buyer cap, pick days, default delivery day.

### ADD A STANDING ORDER

1. Open STANDING ORDERS.
2. Type the buyer, the species, KG per week and the price in colones per kg.
3. Choose the delivery days and the date it starts (a Monday).
4. Tick the confirmed box once the buyer has agreed.
5. Press SAVE STANDING ORDER.

### GOOD TO KNOW

Changing kilograms, price or day starts a new version on a Monday and keeps the old one for the history. Delivering a line on the pick list makes a paid sale in TRADE.

## MONEY (16)

*Who owes you, receipts, spending, profit and loss, cash flow and what-ifs.*

### THE TABS

- **MONEY OWED**: Every unpaid sale by buyer and age. Mark one paid or written off.
- **RECEIPTS**: A numbered receipt for a sale, to copy and send.
- **SPENDING + CASH IN**: Money out, and money in that is not a sale (loans, workshops).
- **PROFIT AND LOSS**: Income minus costs by month, in colones and US dollars.
- **12-MONTH CASH FLOW**: Planned and real cash in and out for twelve months, month by month.
- **BUDGET**: The monthly lines you plan. PUT THESE IN ALL 12 MONTHS copies one month across.
- **SALES TRENDS**: Which species and buyers are rising or falling.
- **WASTE LOG**: Mushrooms dried, donated, composted or thrown out.
- **TRACE + RECALL**: Type a lot to see every buyer who got it, or a buyer to see their lots.
- **WHAT-IF PLANNER**: Change bags, price or costs and see profit and the break-even point.
- **GUESSES**: The figures the app guessed (age bands, how much power grows with bags, and so on) for you to check.

### MARK A SALE PAID

1. Open MONEY OWED.
2. Tap the buyer's unpaid sale.
3. Type the day the cash came in and press the save button. The buyer comes off hold when nothing else is owed.

### GOOD TO KNOW

Electricity in the books comes from ICE BILLS in LEDGER. Stock deliveries and logged hours count as spending unless you switch them off, so they are not counted twice. Owner only.

## ASK THE LATTICE (22)

*The farm assistant: ask in plain words, or tell it something to save.*

### ASK A QUESTION

1. Type in the box, or at the > prompt: ASK words, or anything ending in ?
2. The answer comes only from your own records. It ends with a button for the screen it came from.
3. Try the EXAMPLES tab: tap one to ask it.

### SAVE BY SENTENCE

1. Type for instance: log 4.2 kg from B1020, or power out.
2. A card shows what it understood. Nothing is saved yet.
3. Press CONFIRM (or type YES) to save, or CANCEL.
4. A saved change is one UNDO step and shows in HISTORY as made by the assistant.

### THE BRAIN

Without setup a rule-based brain answers the usual sentences quickly. On the ENGINE tab you can point the assistant at a language model running on a farm computer (Ollama, llama.cpp or LM Studio). It is never a cloud service. If the model is off or slow the rules answer.

### GOOD TO KNOW

Staff get no money answers and no buyer names. Questions it cannot answer from the records get a short refusal and a line on what you can ask.

- **ONE RECORD**: The whole life of one record. Open it from any record's history button.

## DATA (20)

*Backups, spreadsheets and restoring.*

### MAKE A BACKUP

1. Open BACK UP.
2. Press SAVE EVERYTHING (ZIP). A file downloads: the backup, a README and one spreadsheet for every table that has records. (BACKUP FILE ONLY (.JSON) downloads just the backup file.)
3. Move the file somewhere safe: a USB stick, your e-mail, another computer.
4. Do it every week at least. The main menu warns you when it is old.

### OTHER PAGES

- **EXPORT SPREADSHEETS**: One table as a CSV file you can open in a spreadsheet.
- **RESTORE**: Put a backup back.
- **GUESSES**: The stale-backup rule and other guessed numbers.

### RESTORE A BACKUP

1. Open RESTORE and pick the backup file.
2. The app shows what would change: how many records are added, changed or removed.
3. Choose ADD AND UPDATE (KEEP THE REST) or the exact restore. A RESTORE button appears with the number of changes.
4. Press it. It asks SURE? TAP AGAIN: press it a second time to really restore.
5. Everything restored is one UNDO step.

### GOOD TO KNOW

Restoring needs the file from the same app. A file from anywhere else is refused. Owner only.

## WORDS AND SHORT FORMS

*What the farm words and codes mean.*

### THE LIST

- **BE%**: Fresh kg picked divided by dry kg of substrate, times 100.
- **BLOCK**: One bag of substrate with spawn.
- **CART**: A rack of blocks that moves together. It has a name like C-07.
- **COGS**: Cost of goods sold: what the mushrooms cost to make.
- **FLUSH**: One wave of mushrooms from a block.
- **G1, G2**: First and second grain spawn generations.
- **ICE**: Costa Rica's electricity utility. Its bill has a charge for energy and one for the highest demand.
- **KNOCK**: The day blocks are inoculated.
- **LOT**: Blocks knocked on the same day. Code like B1020.
- **P0, P1, P2**: Steps away from the stored master culture.
- **RH**: Relative humidity, in percent.
- **SLANT, WATER TUBE**: Ways to store a culture for months.
- **T/E TEST**: Test and expand: grow a test batch before using a stored culture in production.
- **75X**: The small sterilizer used in the lab.

## IF SOMETHING GOES WRONG

*Common problems and what to try.*

### MY CHANGE IS WRONG

Press UNDO right away. If it was a while ago, open HISTORY and put the old value back.

### THE DATA LOOKS EMPTY ON A DIFFERENT DEVICE

In local mode each device keeps its own records. Make a backup on the device that has them (DATA) and restore it on the other one.

### A BUTTON DOES NOTHING WHEN I TAP IT

Tip mode may be on. The ? TIPS button in the status line is lit. Tap it to turn tip mode off.

### I CANNOT OPEN A SCREEN

The answer bar above the prompt says why. A staff login cannot open money screens, history, data or the textbook. Ask the owner.

### THE SCREEN IS HARD TO READ

Press F6 for CLEAN mode in sunlight, or F5 to change the colour, or open TUNE and lower the glass strength.

### THERE IS NO SOUND

Sound plays only when text appears, and phones only allow it after a tap. Check F4 shows MUTE (sound is on) and that the phone is not on silent.

### THE LAMP SAYS WAITING OR CONFLICT

WAITING means changes are saved on this device and still to be sent. CONFLICT means two devices changed the same record: open HISTORY, then SAVING STATUS, to see what was kept.
